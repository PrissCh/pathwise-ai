PathWise AI
AI-Powered Smart Property Safety Ecosystem

1. Project Overview
PathWise AI is an integrated smart property safety platform designed to enhance pedestrian safety, improve emergency response coordination, and support efficient property management within mixed-use developments.
The platform brings together residents, security personnel, and property management through three interconnected dashboards.
By combining safety-focused navigation, proactive safety monitoring, emergency assistance, and facility management, PathWise AI aims to create a safer and more connected environment.

Our Vision: To transform traditional property management into a proactive, integrated, and safety-oriented ecosystem.

2. Problem Statement
Residents and visitors may face various safety risks when navigating residential and commercial properties, especially during nighttime.

Key Challenges

a. Unsafe Walking Environments
- Poorly lit pedestrian walkways
- Limited CCTV coverage
- Isolated pedestrian areas
- Temporary walkway closures

b. Delayed Emergency Assistance
- Difficulty requesting immediate security assistance
- Limited communication between residents and security personnel
- Delayed identification of potential safety incidents

c. Reactive Safety Monitoring
- Traditional safety systems often depend on users manually reporting emergencies.
- Potential incidents may go unnoticed when users are unable to request assistance.

d. Fragmented Property Management
- Safety incidents and facility issues may be handled through separate processes.
- Limited coordination between residents, security personnel, and property management can delay responses.

These challenges highlight the need for a unified platform that connects personal safety, security operations, and property management.

3. Proposed Solution
PathWise AI introduces a smart property safety ecosystem that integrates three user-specific dashboards into one platform.

a. Resident Dashboard
Empowers residents to navigate more safely, request assistance, and receive proactive safety notifications.

b. Security Control Dashboard
Enables security personnel to monitor safety alerts, review reported incidents, and coordinate emergency responses.

c. Property Management Dashboard
Allows property management to oversee facility conditions, manage incidents, and support a safer property environment.

Our Core Concept:
One Integrated Platform. Three Connected Dashboards. One Safer Community.

4. Key Features

Feature 1: Smart Safe Route Recommendation
Traditional navigation systems typically prioritise the shortest or fastest route.
PathWise AI introduces safety-focused route recommendations by considering multiple environmental risk factors.

Safety Factors:
- Lighting conditions
- CCTV coverage
- Security checkpoint proximity
- Reported safety incidents
- Walkway accessibility
- Route distance

The platform demonstrates how users can compare route options based on safety considerations rather than distance alone.

Feature 2: Emergency Assistance
Residents can request security assistance through the platform.
Workflow:
1. Resident activates the emergency assistance function.
2. An assistance request is generated.
3. The Security Control Dashboard receives the alert.
4. Security personnel review the request.
5. Security personnel coordinate the appropriate response.
The prototype demonstrates how emergency communication can be streamlined through a connected platform.

Feature 3: Proactive Safety Check-In
PathWise AI introduces a proactive safety monitoring mechanism designed to identify situations that may require attention.
Demonstration Scenario:
1. A resident remains at the same location for an extended period.
2. The system triggers a safety check-in notification.
3. The resident is asked: **"Are you safe?"**
4. The resident has **45 seconds** to select **"I'm Safe."**
5. If no response is received, the system automatically generates an emergency alert.
6. The alert is escalated to the Security Control Dashboard for further action.

**Key Innovation:**
The system demonstrates a shift from reactive emergency reporting toward proactive safety monitoring.

6. Innovation & Unique Value Proposition

a. Safety-First Navigation
PathWise AI prioritises safety-related environmental factors rather than focusing exclusively on travel distance.

b. Proactive Safety Monitoring
The platform demonstrates automated safety check-ins and escalation mechanisms that may help identify situations requiring assistance.

c. Integrated Security Response
Emergency alerts are connected to a dedicated Security Control Dashboard, supporting more coordinated responses.

d. Connected Property Management
Facility management is integrated into the wider safety ecosystem, allowing property-related risks to be addressed through structured incident workflows.

e. Unified Digital Ecosystem
Rather than operating as separate applications, PathWise AI combines resident services, security monitoring, and property management within one platform.

8. Prototype Scope

PathWise AI is currently a functional web-based prototype designed to demonstrate the proposed safety ecosystem.
Certain features, including real-time location tracking, AI-driven risk prediction, emergency escalation, and facility control, may require additional infrastructure and integration for real-world deployment.

9. Project Purpose

PathWise AI was developed as a hackathon prototype to explore how digital technologies can enhance pedestrian safety, emergency response, and property management within mixed-use developments.
The project aims to demonstrate the potential of an integrated safety ecosystem that connects residents, security control, and property management.
